Provider Overview

OVHcloud is a French cloud provider and one of the largest dedicated server hosts in Europe. It designs and runs its own servers and data centers, which is a big part of why its prices are low for real bare metal. Its dedicated servers come in tiers: Kimsufi and the SYS range for budget machines, Rise for general-purpose servers, Game for game hosting, and Advance and Scale for heavier production work.

In February 2026, OVHcloud launched its Bare Metal 2026 line-up built on the latest AMD Ryzen and EPYC processors, refreshing the Rise, Game, Advance and Scale ranges. The budget Kimsufi brand sells older-generation Intel Xeon and AMD hardware at very low monthly prices, with anti-DDoS protection included and no long-term commitment.

Features & Technical Architecture Benefits

Even the cheapest OVHcloud servers come with unmetered bandwidth. Budget Kimsufi and SYS-1 machines have 500 Mbps public ports, Rise and Game servers get 1 Gbps, and the Advance-5 offers 3 Gbps public plus 25 Gbps of private networking. Many models include OVHcloud's vRack private network, so you can link several servers over a private connection.

Anti-DDoS protection is included with every Kimsufi Black Friday offer at no extra cost, which is useful for game servers and other public-facing workloads. Kimsufi servers are available in France, Germany, the UK, Poland, Canada, India, Singapore and Australia, and the US-only deals use OVHcloud's Vint Hill, Virginia and Hillsboro, Oregon data centers.

These are self-managed servers. You get root access and can install the operating system of your choice, but OVHcloud doesn't administer the software for you, so you'll need Linux or Windows server skills or a separate control panel.
