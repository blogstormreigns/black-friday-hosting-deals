Provider Overview

Database Mart is a US hosting provider that focuses on low-cost dedicated servers, along with VPS and GPU servers. It's built for buyers who want a physical machine at close to VPS prices. Its servers run in Tier 2 or higher US data centers staffed 24/7, and customers get a control panel for reboots, OS reinstalls, monitoring and backups.

The trade-off for the low prices is hardware generation: many of the cheapest servers use older Intel Xeon E3 processors with modest network ports. For a small business site, a dev or staging box, or a database server that needs dedicated disks rather than raw CPU speed, that's often a good deal. Database Mart offers a 99.9% uptime guarantee and 24/7 support, though it doesn't currently offer phone support.

Features & Technical Architecture Benefits

Setup is quick for dedicated hardware: Database Mart says servers are usually ready in 20 to 40 minutes, though it can take up to 48 hours when stock is short. Every server is a single-tenant physical machine, so you're not sharing CPU or disk I/O with other customers the way you would on a VPS.

The lifetime-plan option is the standout. Most dedicated server discounts disappear after the first term, while a lifetime plan keeps the Black Friday price for as long as you keep the server, which makes long-term costs easy to predict.

The management panel covers the basic jobs, like reboots, reinstalls, monitoring and backups. For anything more, such as a web control panel or a tuned database stack, plan on setting it up yourself.
