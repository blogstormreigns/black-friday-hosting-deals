Black Friday Hosting Deals: FAQ

When do Black Friday web hosting deals start and end in 2026?

Most hosting Black Friday sales started in early to mid-November 2026. The earliest end on November 28–30 (Database Mart, Cloudways, Kinsta, DreamHost, KnownHost), and many run into early December. The latest ones end on December 7–8 (WPX, HostArmada, HostPapa, ChemiCloud). The Ends column in the table above shows the end date for each host.

Do I need a promo code for these hosting deals?

Most deals listed here are applied automatically through the deal link. Where a host needs a code, such as Cloudways, WP Engine or A2 Hosting, the code is shown in the comparison table and on that host's deal page.

Will my hosting renew at the Black Friday price?

Usually not. Most discounts apply to the first billing term only, and the plan renews at the regular rate. Exceptions are noted on each host's page, such as ServerMania's lifetime discount and Database Mart's lifetime plans. Always check the renewal price at checkout.
